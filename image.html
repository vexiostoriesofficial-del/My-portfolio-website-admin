Upload Image
Supported: JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP
Recommended maximum size: 5 MB
How it works
1. Select Profile or Promotion.
2. Choose your image.
3. Press Upload Image.
4. The image will be uploaded to Supabase Storage.
5. A public image URL will be generated.
6. You can use that URL in Profile/Promotion Manager.
